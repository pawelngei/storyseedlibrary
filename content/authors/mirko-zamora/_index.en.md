I am an Argentine illustrator and concept artist, largely self-taught. My journey began searching for answers I couldn't find in traditional education: a way to build worlds, characters, and environments capable of telling stories with a strong identity.

Over the years, I have collaborated with psychoanalysis journals, independent authors, companies, and my own narrative projects, developing a practice that combines research, design, and visual storytelling.

Today, thanks to Story Seed Library, I am putting onto paper years of imagining possible futures for the Argentine landscape: architecture, communities, and ecosystems where technology develops in harmony with natural cycles, guided by respect and responsibility. Solar Punk Criollo was born from this collaboration and the shared desire to help imagine a better future for our civilization.
