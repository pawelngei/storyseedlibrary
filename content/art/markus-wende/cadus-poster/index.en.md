## CADUS International Disaster Response

CADUS is a non-profit, independent, international emergency response organization from Berlin. In our projects, we provide rapid, needs-oriented emergency aid and promote long-term capacity building on the ground.

We follow humanitarian principles and rely on local cooperation. We work together on creative solutions and share them publicly. Our commitment focuses on four areas: Emergency aid, cooperation, innovation and information.

Commissioned Work for [Cadus e.V.](https://www.cadus.org/en/)
