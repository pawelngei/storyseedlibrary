This art was a submission to the
[RealUtopien](https://realutopien.info/visuals/earth-college-utopia-2048/)
project.

The image was commissioned for Lino Zeddies' book “Utopia 2048” to depict a
utopian university campus, the “Earth College” in the year 2048. Based on
satellite images from Google Earth from 2020, the cityscape of Malmö was
modified to represent a futuristic university city of sustainability,
beauty, and vitality.

Aerroscape, aka Alexander Rommel, works in landscape architecture and urban
planning and is a part-time digital painter. Through art, he tries to raise
awareness of the importance of respecting nature and the environment.
