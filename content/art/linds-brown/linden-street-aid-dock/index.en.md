I wanted to show how my street could respond to a flood if one were to happen tomorrow in my town in Louisiana, USA. 

On June 15, we experienced a lot of flooding in town and while the water did not get as high as in my illustration it has made me think about how I will need to work with neighbors to provide food, water, sandbags, a charging station, a med station, a washing area, and of course, a social area.

A lot of people where I live keep boats. I imagine people would float around to help people and to go in search of aid.

During Hurricaine Katrina in 2005, a number of people down south formed an ad hoc group called, "The Cajun Navy" and worked outside (but sometimes with) the government to help those in the flooding. The group has splintered and some of the groups are non-profits now, but there is precedent for a flotilla of Louisiana Solarpunks at some point in the future.
