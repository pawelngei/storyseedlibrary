Solarpunk scene on a sunny wheat field, where some small robots do the harvest while a kid is reading on a woman's lap. The community works on building wind energy while also having some free time to enjoy themselves. All three elements of the title are reunited and sourced sustainably in the image.

The [illustration](https://xuliavicente.com/project/utopies/) was created for the exhibition Utopies, curated by [APIV – The Illustrators’ Association of Valencia](https://apiv.com/).
