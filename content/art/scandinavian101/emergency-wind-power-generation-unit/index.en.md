Following a tsunami, the affected community is slowly gathering and rebuilding, but to provide immediate power supply, this unit was deployed. Several floating power turbines(with colorful schemes painted by their crews) are connected to a mobile battery unit, which then provides power to the community. A camp for the displaced people and a several basic medical facilities are directly receiving the power.
