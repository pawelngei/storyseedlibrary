A sketch of a sustainable future - a train filled with small containers with
food from local farming cooperatives.

It was used by [alxd](/authors/alxd) in his [Lenses and Foundations
essay](https://alxd.org/solarpunk-lenses-and-foundations.html) to illustrate
how hard it is to explain actual sustainable practices on an infrastructural
scale without symbols encoded in the culture.
