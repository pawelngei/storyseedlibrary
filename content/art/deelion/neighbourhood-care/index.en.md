It's the third flood in as many years, but these neighbours have built a pretty resilient local system to prepare themselves.

Basement apartment residents are boxing up their treasured memories and preparing to catch the last community bus service across town, or for sleepovers with their upstairs neighbours.

Projector drones, equipped with latest forecasts, are followed around by the local children brandishing chalk, marking up the walls so the sandbag teams know how high to stack before moving onto the next house.

The local communication network becomes a community aid message board, so assistance is never far away, and since the shared tool sheds were built on every street last year, responders can travel light and work fast.

They've learned to rely on each other, and they know they'll be okay because, as the graffitied old Neighbourhood Watch poster in the window says: this neighbourhood cares.
