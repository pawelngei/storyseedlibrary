This is the first of a series of illustrations I'll be posting in the coming months! It'll be about Brazilian Indigenous Futurism or as I'm calling it "Amazofuturism"! It was inspired by the different cultures and art of various Indigenous peoples of Brazil, like the Kayapós, the Tukanos, Barés and Guaranis. I was also inspired by solarpunk and afrofuturism!
