It is the Early to mid summer in Southeastern Virginia during hurricane season. In order to prepare, members of the community would forage for edible and medicinal plants ready to harvest. They would split off into groups of two or three based on whatever edible plant they were supposed to find. Each person carries a zine that helps with Identifying the plants they are looking for. 

In this scene, these two are looking for cattails.

Plants included:

Oaks, Pines, and sycamores in the background as well as cattails and pickerelweed.
