On the left is a plein air painting of evening traffic as viewed from the
Morrissey Blvd overpass. On the right is a speculative repaint of our future
society. Shown is the Hwy 1 High Speed Rail at the center divider beside a
transfer station to the prolific local bus line. A line of native trees
divides the road from the rail. Through trial and error the community has
settled on a permit system allowing only occupational trucks to use the
roads with the busses. Vehicles are adorned with murals by local artists
instead of ads.
