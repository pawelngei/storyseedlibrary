In a sustainable tomorrow technology is created cooperatively and shared freely around the world - and some such projects exist today! The [Open Source Hardware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-source_hardware) movement promotes collaborative creation and distribution of even the most complex devices without locking them with patents or intellectual property.

[OpenFlexure](https://openflexure.org/) is a great example of it - an automated digital microscope which can be used in both scientific and medical environments - and can be 3D-printed anywhere in the world! It was initially designed at United Kingdom universities, but was then tested, used and improved by a community now spanning over 70 countries! It was assessed for malaria research in [Tanzania](https://openflexure.org/about/malaria), used to measure soil health in [Argentina](https://openflexure.org/openflexurecon2022/community_stories/2022/10/20/openflexurecon-castro.html) and water quality monitoring in [Ukraine](https://www.appropedia.org/Tolocar/OpenFlexure_Microscope_in_Ukraine).

You can read more about similar open projects at [Appropedia](https://www.appropedia.org/), which hosts multiple collaboratively developed sustainable tech projects!
