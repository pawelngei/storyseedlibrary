Leave the leaves! It is time to silence the leaf blowers and gas-chugging lawnmowers and let our ecosystem (and our ears!) rest and heal. Noise pollution is still pollution. Did you know that beneath your feet is an entire biome that uses leaf litter as circular currency? 

Here’s how it works. Decomposers like bacteria, protozoa and earthworms break down fallen leaves into nitrate rich mulch, turning leaf piles into a 24/7 café to feed the biome. Hungry trees and plants need to feed on this hearty stew of organic compost during the winter months and build up reserves. Then, during spring and summer the trees will trade this sugar with mushrooms in exchange for phosphorus and chemical signals to talk to their tree friends. Mushrooms cannot actually make their own food, but are fed this leafy ‘soup’ by their guardian trees. The roots turn into a massive farmers market, swapping meals for information and elements. The trees can then grow stronger and taller and healthier, and in the fall drop carbon filled leaves back to the floor to become a rich slurry, and the cycle begins again. 

When we blow and throw away the leaves, we starve our backyard ecosystem of currency, shelter, nutrition and information. Fall leaves replenish our eroding topsoil without the use of synthetic fertilizers. If you want bees, butterflies, fireflies, salamanders, mushrooms and healthy trees in your neighborhood it’s time to hang up the leafblower and leave the leaves! They are called LEAVES after all. 

We can still keep the sidewalks and walkways clear of leaves for our communities to safely walk on, but leave the leaves in our yards for our ecosystem to heal.
