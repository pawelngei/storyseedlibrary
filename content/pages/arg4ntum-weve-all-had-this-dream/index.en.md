We've all had this dream

We all sort of remember

Some reach for it still, while others choose to surrender

You know, the one with all of the feelings you wish you knew how to describe?

The one you think about all the time

It feels similar and so afflicting, i know it, believe

You can't go a day without choking away the need to grieve

A memory you can almost recall

A faint feeling you had last fall

Nearly on the tip of your tongue, it spins

You just need to catch it before the other unbearable loop begins

It's a slippery slope going to the edge of your imagination

But it's so worth it, you betcha

Feeling pride for the ride you had to endure

The presence of life well lived right as you go through that door

The memories come rushing in for a hug

You feel like the rest was just a myth or a strange system bug

Finally, feel you're home for the long run

Knowing that you fully belong now.
